# Liquidity Reporting Overview

Treasury analysts prepare end-of-day liquidity reports by reconciling positions, expected cash flows, and collateral values from multiple internal systems. The objective is to understand current liquidity capacity and identify any risks to near-term funding and collateral needs.

- Source data must be checked for freshness before a report is finalized.
- The analyst should preserve source record IDs to support traceability.
- Any unusual ratio or outlier should be reviewed before release.
- This repository is a training-focused demo and not a production banking system.
